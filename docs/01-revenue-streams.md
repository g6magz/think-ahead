## 1. Streams that have numbers

### 1.1 Sale (or financed sale) of the solar shade system

Planning case from the shade-system draft, recommended price **$172,500**:

| Item | Amount | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| Delivered cost (factory + install), base | $112,100 | Estimate |
| Gross margin (35%) | $60,400 | Estimate at that price |
| Customer acquisition (solar-channel analog, $0.84/W × 12 kW) | $10,080 | Industry analog, not a measured Think Ahead CAC |
| Warranty reserve (4% of price) | $6,900 | Estimate |
| Contribution margin after CAC and warranty | **$43,420 (25.2%)** | Estimate. Does **not** subtract the cost of funding 0% loans |
| Price at 25% / 45% gross margin on the same base cost | $149,500 / $203,800 | Illustration |

Federal and Xcel incentives are counted as **zero** in these unit economics for a 2026 install. Someone still has a cost of capital on the 0% book; that subsidy is called out as unmodeled in the source.

This $43,420 figure is the per-deal margin for **selling the shade system**. In the later 2×-retail ownership model, the source says this margin belongs to selling the shade system and is not counted again as energy-spread profit.

### 1.2 Insurance commissions (optional attach)

Planning rates used in the source: **15% new, 12% renewal**. On the $5,511 starting premium path:

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Year-1 new-business commission if written at the full $5,511 average | $827 |
| Year-1 commission if the year-1 program premium is already 10% off ($4,960) | $744 |
| Renewal at the year-2 program premium ($4,464) | $536 |
| Renewal at the year-10 program premium ($1,922) | $231 |

A 10-year commission projection in section 10.6 of the shade draft uses a **placeholder** install ramp (100 systems in year 1, rising to 1,700 in year 10; 8,350 systems over 10 years), a **60% attach rate**, and **90%/year persistency** (those last two are estimates). Rounded 10-year commission total: **about $4.91 million**. Year-10 commission revenue in that table: **$953,000**. Year-10 in-force policies: 3,880.

This is not a board-approved volume plan. Insurance cannot be required as a condition of the loan or the system under Colorado anti-tying / anti-rebating rules cited in the source (C.R.S. §§ 10-3-1105, 10-2-602, 10-2-606, and related).

If Think Ahead were the **insurer** rather than an agency, year-10 in-force premium (before losses) at $1,922 × 3,880 would be about **$7.5 million**. The source calls that a different, risk-bearing business and does not mix it into the commission total.

### 1.3 Hypothetical 2×-retail offtake (Think Ahead owns the system)

**CONCEPT STAGE as a contracted rate: no Colorado utility pays 2× retail today.** The arithmetic below is from section 15 of the whole-home payback draft, using 15¢ retail and 30¢ offtake, a typical 7,008 kWh/year Xcel bill, and no home battery:

| | 30% efficiency (117,830 kWh, $725,500 system) | 33% efficiency (129,640 kWh, $797,500 system) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Revenue at 30¢/kWh | $35,349/year | $38,892/year |
| Bill paid back to the homeowner | $1,051/year | $1,051/year |
| Spread Think Ahead keeps | $34,298/year | $37,841/year |
| Net after estimated solar upkeep | $31,305/year | $34,548/year |
| Simple payback from the spread | about 21 years (about 23 after upkeep) | about 21 years (about 23 after upkeep) |

Insurance commissions in that section shorten payback by only about half a year. The $43,420 shade-system contribution margin is not added on top of this spread.

**Post-payback split (computed in chat on November 23, 2026; not yet committed as Section 16 of the payback file).** After payback, the same power still earns the same revenue. What changes is that the yearly net after upkeep ($31,305 at 30%, $34,548 at 33%) becomes cash to split. Three illustrations were calculated, using the $1,922 year-10+ premium as the “insurance covered” add-on. Panel wear-down (typically about 0.5% per year) was not applied; the source note was that this would make post-payback numbers about 10–12% lower. Totals below are simple multiples, not a discounted cash flow.

| Split after payback | Homeowner per year | Think Ahead per year | Homeowner over 10 / 18 years | Think Ahead over 10 / 18 years |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 30%, bill only | $1,051 | $31,305 | $10,510 / $18,918 | $313,050 / $563,490 |
| 30%, bill plus remaining insurance premium covered | $2,973 | $29,383 | $29,730 / $53,514 | $293,830 / $528,894 |
| 30%, bill plus half the profit | $16,704 | $15,653 | $167,040 / $300,672 | $156,530 / $281,754 |
| 33%, bill only | $1,051 | $34,548 | $10,510 / $18,918 | $345,480 / $621,864 |
| 33%, bill plus remaining insurance premium covered | $2,973 | $32,626 | $29,730 / $53,514 | $326,260 / $587,268 |
| 33%, bill plus half the profit | $18,325 | $17,274 | $183,250 / $329,850 | $172,740 / $310,932 |

With upkeep counted, payback lands closer to **year 23** than year 21. None of this works unless a buyer actually pays double retail.

## 2. Streams stated as concepts, with no revenue model

**CONCEPT STAGE: no supporting model yet**

- Invest Ahead itself is a financing structure (0% APR, intended to roll into the first mortgage, plus a credit-free in-house layer). It is a **use of capital**, not a priced revenue line. Cost of funds is unmodeled.
- Product tiers Jason stated on November 23–25, 2026 (buy now and invest in data centers; let us pay your electric bill; own your system and sell power back to Think Ahead) — see [docs/11-storage-network-and-tiers.md](11-storage-network-and-tiers.md). No prices, shares, or take rates were set for those tiers.
- Autonomous bidding / “blue-link-on-the-bill” sales flow: **no notes were found** in the repo or Drive folder. The shade draft models a software-led (“autonomous”) funnel only as a CAC analog ($10,080 per 12 kW system) and a 5% close-rate planning case.
- Upfront money-down option: **no notes were found**.
- RTD overnight network, HOV fines, $100K DUI penalty, and other program fines or fares: no fee schedule or revenue model. See [docs/07-programs.md](07-programs.md).
- Seven side businesses: count stated in the bot profile; none named and none modeled. See [docs/02-side-businesses.md](02-side-businesses.md).
