## 1. Core concept

Think Ahead is described as a Colorado public-health investment program. The original repository README and the project bot’s description both place it at the intersection of impaired-driving prevention, insurance-rate reduction, a solar and hail retrofit ecosystem, Invest Ahead financing, and an RTD overnight ride network.

The bot profile states the following scope, quoted in substance and not expanded:

- Impaired-driving prevention
- Insurance premium reduction of 10% per year for 10 years, to about $1,100
- Hail protection systems: blankets, autonomous shade screens, solar carports, and a total solar roof with snow-melt pulses
- Invest Ahead: 0% APR financing rolled into the first mortgage
- RTD overnight ride network, 10 PM–6 AM
- Impaired-driver hazard protocol
- HOV slow-lane fines
- $100K DUI penalty
- 13-state Hail Alley expansion
- 7 side businesses
- 18-year exit at $750M–$9B
- Ballot initiative logistics
- Contractor network

Of that list, **only the hail-protection / solar product, the insurance-premium arithmetic, Invest Ahead financing, and a 13-state Hail Alley list have written analysis** in the business-plan files. The driving, ballot, contractor-roster, side-business, and exit items remain concept-stage. See [GAPS.md](../GAPS.md).

## 2. What has been modeled (November 23, 2026 drafts)

Work in late November 2026 was concentrated on product pricing and electricity payback for a Front Range / Denver planning home.

**Solar shade system** (typical planning case: 2,000 sq ft A-frame roof, 24 kW of solar fabric, 12 kW collecting at a time):

- Delivered cost, base estimate: **$112,100** (factory **$80,100** + install **$32,000**). Planning range about $57,500–$205,200.
- Recommended customer price: **$172,500** at a 35% gross margin (**about $60,400**).
- After estimated sales cost ($10,080) and a 4% warranty reserve ($6,900): contribution margin **$43,420**, or **25.2%**.
- The cost of funding the 0% loans is **not** subtracted from that margin.
- Energy-only payoff on the 0% loan at the $172,500 price: about **75 years** in the base case.
- With the insurance-premium path bundled (optional, and it cannot be required as a condition of the loan under Colorado law): about **32 years** in the base case (about 28–36 years across the low-to-high cost band).
- The 30% federal residential solar tax credit (IRC §25D) ended for home installs after December 31, 2025. These figures do not count on it.

**Insurance premium path.** The program target is a 10% reduction each year for 10 years, “to about $1,100.” Starting from the cited 2026 Colorado average HO-3 premium of **$5,511**, ten 10% cuts land at about **$1,922**, not $1,100. A path that ends at $1,100 would need a starting premium of about $3,155. Both facts are kept: the stated program language, and the arithmetic from the cited average. The 10%/year schedule is a program target, not how Colorado homeowners policies are rated today.

**Whole-home five-piece package** (roof with snow-melt, ground-length shade screens, garage roll-out shades, back gazebo, front carport):

- About **100 kW** at 30% module efficiency and **110 kW** at 33% (117,830 / 129,640 kWh per year after snow-melt, estimates).
- Base delivered cost: **$725,500** / **$797,500**.
- If every kilowatt-hour were credited at 15¢: about **41-year** simple payback at both efficiencies.
- Under Xcel’s real consumption cap (leftover power at about 1.16¢/kWh): about **278 / 290 years**.
- Fastest component on an all-retail 15¢ basis at 30%: the front carport, about **15.5 years**. None of the five pieces, or the package, pays back inside 10 years in the current numbers.
- Jason’s later offtake test (section 15 of the payback draft): sell all output at **2× retail**, rebate the homeowner’s full electric bill (about **$1,051/year** on a typical 7,008 kWh Xcel use), and keep the spread. At 30¢ offtake with no home battery, Think Ahead’s spread is about **$34,298 / $37,841** per year and simple payback from the spread is about **21 years** (about **23 years** after estimated solar upkeep). **No Colorado utility pays 2× retail today.** Xcel’s leftover credit is about 1.16¢/kWh. This case is hypothetical.

**Homeowner benefit used in that 2×-retail ownership model (Think Ahead owns the system):** about **$103,000 over 25 years** — $26,280 of electric bills paid ($1,051 × 25) plus $76,647 of insurance savings as the premium falls from $5,511 to about $1,922. That 25-year span is a standard solar-life horizon. It is **not** tied to a calendar start date.
