# Think Ahead — 13-State Hail Alley Expansion

**Date:** November 25, 2026 (America/Denver)
**Document type:** Source-verified expansion notes
**Sources:** `business-plan/solar-shade-system.md` (market line, sections 14.2 and 13 source 32); Think Ahead Investment Project bot profile

---

## 1. Stated scope

The bot profile and the shade-system draft both describe later expansion from a Denver / Front Range base market to **13 Hail Alley states**.

The shade draft uses the 13-state “hail belt” list from a Moody’s insurance-industry hail study:

**Arkansas, Colorado, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, Oklahoma, South Dakota, Texas, and Wisconsin.**

The same draft notes that NOAA’s narrower geographic “Hail Alley” is the area where Nebraska, Colorado, and Wyoming meet. **Wyoming is in that geography but not in the 13-state list**, and only part of Texas is high-hail.

---

## 2. Demand notes that exist (still not an expansion plan)

From shade-system section 14.2 (November 23, 2026):

- Front Range housing units, Colorado State Demography Office Vintage 2024 (10-county core): **2,074,191**. A broader corridor adding Pueblo, Elbert, and Teller: **2,174,386**.
- Census Bureau housing-unit estimates, July 1, 2025 (V2025), as totaled in shade-system section 14.2: **39,807,104** housing units across those 13 states (about 40 million; Texas is nearly one-third). The state-by-state table lives in that file.
- Order-of-magnitude Front Range geometric pool after rough single-family and gable filters: about **0.7–0.9 million roofs**, before owner-occupancy, HOA, structural, and willingness screens. The placeholder ramp of 100 to 1,700 systems a year is described as well under 1% of that pool.

## 3. What is still concept-stage

**CONCEPT STAGE: no supporting expansion model yet** for:

- Order of states after Colorado
- Regulatory and insurance-filing work in each state
- Crew bases, factories, or capital by state
- A timeline

The scale section only says that by years 5–10, labor is the likely constraint unless training and multi-state crews are underway, and it lists “Hail Alley crew bases” as a year-10 fix.
