The year-10 premium is about **$1,922, not $1,100**. The “to about $1,100” language is retained as a stated program target. A 10% path that ends at $1,100 would need a starting premium of about $3,155, which would produce about $13,055 of 10-year cumulative savings and about $43,900 over 25 years.

Year one of this clock has **no written calendar start date** as of November 25, 2026.

## 3. What a 10-year projection does **not** exist for

- Program-wide revenue, cost, and profit (shade sales + insurance + offtake + programs + side businesses)
- Invest Ahead cost of funds and credit losses
- Whole-home package volumes or a 10-year offtake P&L
- Ballot, RTD, HOV, or DUI program finances
- An exit or valuation at year 10 or year 15 (an external November 25, 2026 portfolio roll-up recorded Think Ahead’s exit as unmodeled, $0, and warned not to convert the $4.91 million commission path into an exit)

Payback periods for the products themselves are all **longer than 10 years** in the current numbers. See [docs/00-executive-summary.md](00-executive-summary.md) and the business-plan files.
