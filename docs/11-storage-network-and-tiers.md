## 1. Offtake and facilities, as stated

Jason’s direction, stated across November 23–25, 2026:

- Store excess power at battery-storage locations and data-storage centers.
- **Think Ahead would be the data center** and would **guarantee the sale** in order to be a **carbon-negative facility**.
- Offer sites **first to prisons** (he pointed at the separate prison project), **then schools**.
- On schools, he noted that **while school is out, nobody is using the school** (idle capacity, including summer).
- Sell power to the **highest bidders**.
- Homeowners should be **able to identify where they are placed**, with sites **placed within their areas**.

A related, earlier offtake test is already written in section 15 of the whole-home payback draft: sell all solar output at **double the retail rate**, rebate the homeowner’s full electric bill, and keep the spread. That draft is explicit that **no Colorado utility pays 2× retail today** (Xcel leftover credit about 1.16¢/kWh). Getting 27–30¢/kWh would take a direct contract with a storage or data-center buyer, or a new rate approved by the Colorado Public Utilities Commission.

This file does not invent a number of prisons, schools, or regional battery sites, and it does not invent megawatt or acre sizes.

## 2. Product tiers, as stated

Jason said different tiers would include:

1. **Buy now and invest in data centers.**
2. **Let us pay your electric bill.**
3. **Own your own system and sell it back to us.**

No prices, ownership percentages, payback rules, or eligibility rules were attached to those tiers in the sources. The 2×-retail “Think Ahead owns the system and rebates the bill” arithmetic in the payback draft is the closest written relative of tier 2, and it is still hypothetical on the offtake rate.

---

## 3. What would be needed before a site count can be written

Not a plan — a gap list:

- Offtaker rate and contract form (2× retail or another price)
- Whether Think Ahead owns generation, storage, the data center, or all three
- Household volume and kWh exported under each tier
- Battery hours and data-center load assumed per site
- Which prison and school sites, if any, are actually available
- Interconnection, land, and permitting constraints

Until those are set, the answer to “how many would we have to build” cannot be sourced from existing material.
